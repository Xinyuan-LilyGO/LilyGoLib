## Other bundled libraries

* [Adafruit GFX Library@1.12.6](https://github.com/adafruit/Adafruit-GFX-Library) (transitive Adafruit dependency)
* [Adafruit INA219@1.2.3](https://github.com/adafruit/Adafruit_INA219) (optional INA219 voltage, current, and power monitor support)
* [Adafruit SH110X@2.1.15](https://github.com/adafruit/Adafruit_SH110X) (transitive Adafruit dependency)
* [MLX90640@1.0.0](https://github.com/Xinyuan-LilyGO/LilyGoLib-ThirdParty) (optional thermal-camera support)
* [PCMFlow@0.2.1](https://github.com/lbuque/PCMFlow) and [PCMFlowG722@0.2.0](https://github.com/tanakamasayuki/PCMFlowG722) (optional walkie-talkie support; both libraries are required)
* [SparkFun MAX3010x Pulse and Proximity Sensor Library@1.1.2](https://github.com/sparkfun/SparkFun_MAX3010x_Sensor_Library) (optional MAX3010x heart-rate and proximity sensor support)
* [arduino-nofrendo@1.2](https://github.com/lewisxhe/arduino-nofrendo.git) (optional Pager NES support)

The separate ESP32 BLE keyboard/mouse forks, TinyGPSPlus, XPowersLib, and
split NFC forks are not part of the current LilyGoLib-ThirdParty bundle.
